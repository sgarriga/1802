# 1802/BASIC

DUNGEON.BAS - port to RSA BASIC from Commodore PET BASIC
              Dungeon by Brian Sawyer for Cursor #15 Copyright (C) 1981

Dungeon was a precursor to the popular Rogue game. Explore the dungeon, collect gold, fight monsters, gain experience.

Controls - Numeric Keypad, Q or Space, then enter
7 - Up & Left
8 - Up
9 - Up & Right
4 - Left
5 - Rest
6 - Right
1 - Down & Left
2 - Down
3 - Down & Right
Q - Quit
SPACE - toggle 'walk through walls' ability

Walking through walls uses up more energy, and more hit points, than walking through open space.
